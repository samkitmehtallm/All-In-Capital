Kirana owners restock the hard way
StockSathi 2
3 trips a week
Owners travel to the nearest 
wholesale market, losing half 
a day each time.
No credit
Banks do not lend against 
stock. Informal suppliers 
charge 3 to 4% a month.
No price view
Prices vary by distributor and 
by day. Owners rarely know if 
they overpay.
Based on 60 owner interviews in Karimnagar and Warangal, June to July 2026.

Why now
StockSathi 3
UPI everywhere

That gives a payment trail we

Quick-commerce apps are 
pulling customers from small 
stores. Owners now need 
better prices and stock.
Phones and voice
Cheap smartphones and 
local-language voice input let 
owners order without typing.
